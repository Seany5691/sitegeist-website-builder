# Matric Portraits

# A Coming of Age

Life may not be perfect but it does have perfect moments. From your Matric dance to a graduation or a simple celebration of your beauty and strength preserve these perfect moments with a unique photo shoot you can treasure for years to come. Book an individual shoot for one, to celebrate yourself and your uniqueness, or plan to capture the glitz and glam of dressing up for a Matric Dance.

Matric Photo Shoots can be done with an individual, a couple, or a whole group of people. If the group is large we bring 2 photographers with to your photo extravaganza and try our best to give you the paparazzi experience. If the pressure is too high on the day- let's look at dressing you up on another day and doing your shoot in a much more relaxed manner.

Great Photography! Very professional and personal. Felt so comfortable during the shoot.

_**–Charisa Van Heerden**_
