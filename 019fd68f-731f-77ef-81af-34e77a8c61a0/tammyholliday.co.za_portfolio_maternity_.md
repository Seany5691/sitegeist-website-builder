# Maternity

# Elegant Belly Moments Captured

Pregnancy is a magical time in your life that goes by faster than you realise. Don’t let this time pass you by, capture this precious moment with a relaxed, fun photoshoot that brings out the miracle of pregnancy and the start of something new. Have your shoot done in the comfort of my little studio, or on location in Gauteng, or even at your home. Take a combination package and add a pretty little album to round off your memorable moments package.

I do Milk baths, Outdoor and Studio Sessions (Midrand). I have dresses for rent, only should you book your session with me - I also have a selection of flower crowns fabrics and other simple props to make your shoot interesting!

Due to the level of service we received on our maternity shoot and the quality of photos she produced we returned again for our newborn shoot. We were anticipating it to be a bit more difficult than the maternity shoot but with Tammy’s patience it was a bliss. She took the most amazing photos of our little one the photos were edited on time and she kept us informed on every process. We will certainly go back to Tammy for more portraits her diligence, professionalism and friendliness is out of this world.

_**– Elias and Lerato Sithole**_
