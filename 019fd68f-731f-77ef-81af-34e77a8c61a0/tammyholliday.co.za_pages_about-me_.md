# About me

# Fun, Relaxed and Colourful

## Hello, I am Tammy

I am the face behind the images you see here and I hope you can see my huge smile! That is my absolute favourite part of what I do, capturing the happiness within a person, within a family and especially the pure untainted happiness within a child! I will always bring out the silliest laughs, and ask for yours too! I love seeing others in a state of bliss - we all deserve to be content don't we?

I also offer other services/products as I have a small additional range of skills. Take a peak around the site - never be scared to ask if you have a question about something you don't see. I cannot wait to hear from you!

![cake smash, milk bath, maternity, family, matric dance, newborn, portrait photographer.](http://tammyholliday.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Tammy-photo-of-Me-Background.jpg)

## My Approach

With me, you will let your guard down, genuinely laugh and smile and be silly!

My goal as your photographer will be to capture all the real raw moments and all the crazy fun! You'll feel more like a friend than some photographer you hired and that also helps you (and your guests) feel more relaxed and with me you'll actually enjoy having your photo taken!
