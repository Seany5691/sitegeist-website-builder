# Newborn Photography

# A New Life to Celebrate

There are few moments in life as delightful as the arrival of a new baby. Soon enough after those crazy first days, you will want to capture your excitement, and your little one, and share it with your family and friends.

Freeze in time those first months of your precious bundle of joy and hold onto those memories forever with a newborn photography session, a must-have for any proud parents. Ask for a beautiful album to showcase your precious mementos.

I prefer the shoots to take place in my studio as everything I need is ready there. However, for R850 I can bring my studio and setup in your home.

Tammy has an Amazing eye for catching the most breathtaking moments! You don't just see completed pictures, but you can actually FEEL those memories again, and re-live those tresured moments in time! She's very talented in creating incredible photos like no other!

- _**Louise Jenkinson**_
