# Couples

# Let Love Lead

Whether you’re celebrating an engagement, anniversary or just being in love preserve the memories and emotions of your relationship with a beautiful photo shoot that brings out the love two people share in a natural, authentic way. Have your shoot done in the comfort of my little studio, or on location in Gauteng, Ask about adding an album to your package to help showcase (and show off) your special memories.

We really love seeing couples adore each other, sometimes this love is romantic and sometimes its crazy! We can do your shoot on location (your choice, my list), but we can also do this in studio. Think about doing a Couples Cake Smash, Lovey Dovey Paint Splash or simply getting cuddly on our in Studio Bed. These shoots could also be done in the comfort of your home.

Wonderful photographer in every way possible. Thank you for going the extra mile! And thank you for my awesome photos. I love them!

_**-Jenny Le Roux**_
