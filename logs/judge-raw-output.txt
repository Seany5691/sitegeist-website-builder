## FEEDBACK
The palette (butter cream / deep cocoa / gold) and the Playfair-style display serif are genuinely on-brand for an artisan bakery, and the repeated contact panel with the "TODAY" badge in the business-hours table is a genuinely premium detail. But the build currently ships with visible layout bugs that break the "R100,000" bar: the interior-page H1s are clipped by the top of the hero band on services, contact, about and portfolio; the hero cards on home are cut off mid-sentence ("our sp…"); and the "Ready to Order Your Custom Cake?" CTA band renders cream-on-cream and is effectively invisible on every page. Forms rely entirely on placeholder text plus native browser validation tooltips, which reads unfinished rather than professional.

## PRIORITY FIXES
1. [high] components/Hero.tsx (interior pages — services, contact, about, portfolio)
ISSUE: The H1 ("What We Bake", "Let's Talk Cake", "Our Story", "Fresh From Our Ovens") is clipped by the top edge of the hero band and collides with the sticky header.
CHANGE: Give the hero band `min-height: 320px` with `padding-top: 96px` and `overflow: visible`, and set the H1 to `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` so it never exceeds the band at any viewport.

2. [high] components/CtaBand.tsx
ISSUE: "Ready to Order Your Custom Cake?" uses white/cream text on the cream page background — contrast is ~1.2:1 and the copy is unreadable on home, services and mobile.
CHANGE: Fill the band with `#3B1A18` (cocoa) at 0.92 opacity over the ambient image, set the heading to `#FFFFFF` and subhead to `#F5E6C8`; verify 4.5:1 against the actual rendered backdrop.

3. [high] components/HeroCards.tsx / home-hero.css
ISSUE: The three hero service cards are clipped mid-sentence at the hero/section boundary on desktop ("our sp…") and overlap each other and the sticky Call Now bar on 390px.
CHANGE: Remove the negative-margin overlap on mobile (stack cards with 24px gap below the hero), and on desktop either shorten the card copy to 2 lines with `line-clamp: 2` + "Read more" or expand the hero so the cards are fully contained.

4. [high] components/ContactForm.tsx
ISSUE: Inputs have no persistent labels (placeholder-only) and errors render as native browser tooltips ("Please fill out this field.") with only a black border — no error copy, no icon, no `aria-invalid`.
CHANGE: Add always-visible labels above each field (`font-size: 14px; color: #6B4A3A`), and on invalid submit show `1.5px solid #B3261E` border, an inline error message in `#B3261E` with a warning icon, plus `aria-describedby` and `role="alert"`.

5. [medium] app/globals.css — form placeholder contrast
ISSUE: Placeholder text is light beige on cream (~2:1) so the form reads as empty and fails WCAG AA.
CHANGE: Set `::placeholder { color: #8A6B4F; opacity: 1; }` and lighten input background to `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #D9C7A7` for a clear field boundary.

6. [medium] components/ServiceCard.tsx
ISSUE: The yellow "Get a Custom Quote" pill sits on top of the image bottom-right corner and collides with the image edge; on mobile it overlaps the photo and pushes the number badge out of rhythm.
CHANGE: Move the pill below the image inside the text column, aligned left with the H3 and "Get Started" button, or pin it consistently to the card's bottom-right corner with 24px inset.

7. [medium] components/PortfolioGrid.tsx
ISSUE: Masonry columns end unevenly (a single tall orphan in column 1) and gaps are inconsistent between the 4-column desktop and 2-column mobile layouts.
CHANGE: Use CSS `grid-auto-rows: 8px` with a fixed row-span per image or `column-count` with uniform `gap: 24px`, and normalize all thumbnails to 4:5 crops.

8. [low] components/LocationMap.tsx
ISSUE: "Our Location" renders as a blank grey box on home and mobile — looks broken.
CHANGE: Render a styled map embed or a fallback static image with address + "Get Directions" button in the same card treatment as the business-hours panel.

## TARGET CHECKLIST
- All H1s fully contained within their hero band at 390/768/1440 with no clipping
- CTA band text passes 4.5:1 against its background
- Form has persistent visible labels above every input (Name, Email, Phone, Message)
- Form errors are custom-styled: red border + inline message + `aria-invalid` + `role="alert"`
- Placeholder text passes 4.5:1 on the input background
- Hero card copy is never truncated without a "…" / "Read more" affordance
- "Get a Custom Quote" pill placement is identical on every service card
- Portfolio grid gaps and crops are uniform across all three viewports
- Map/location block renders content instead of an empty grey box
- Focus-visible outline (2px, offset 2px) on all buttons, links and inputs
- Icon-only buttons (menu, social, quote) carry `aria-label`
- Tap targets on mobile are ≥ 44px (nav links, FAQ rows, phone number)

## UNIQUENESS
- Replace the generic yellow number circles with hand-drawn icing-swirl SVG numerals piped in gold — a small motif that could run through section labels too.
- Add a "cake cross-section" section divider: an SVG silhouette of a layered cake slice that fills with colour as the user scrolls, used as the transition into the portfolio.
- Give the "TODAY" business-hours row a subtle animated gradient (cream → gold) so the page feels alive in real time; it's the most distinctive element currently and deserves to be the hero of the contact block.
- Turn the "Our Work" grid into a hover-reveal "menu card": each cake image dims and shows the flavour name in the display serif ("Black Forest · Belgian Chocolate"), matching the bakery-counter concept.
- A vertical "recipe card" strip on the About page — a torn-paper edge element with the grandmother's-notebook story typeset as handwriting, honouring the "family recipes" brand line.
- Hero could cycle the three service images with a soft cross-fade behind the display headline instead of the static chocolate-texture backdrop, immediately proving the product.

## STOCK IMAGE NOTES
- Home hero backdrop and interior-page hero bands use a dark chocolate/batter texture stock image — replace with a real wide shot of the Mamma & Mia counter or a signature cake being iced, shot at the same warm cream-toned lighting as the rest of the site.
- The portfolio mixes styles: a moody black-background cupcake, a caramel-pour shot, and a bright pastel studio cake don't read as one bakery. Replace with a consistent set of real product photos on a single neutral surface with the same warm light.
- "Daily Bakery Counter" and the dark bakery-shelf images are generic stock shopfronts — use real photos of the North West counter and display case.
- The cake with "50 & Fabulous" topper and the "Happy Birthday" slate are the only images that feel like real commissions; keep this direction and shoot all portfolio items as real customer orders with the same framing.
- Testimonial avatars use letter monograms (S, D, S) — fine as placeholders, but swap for real customer initials/photos once consent is obtained.
- The marble-and-gold "Custom Cake Consultation" image is stock and over-styled versus the brand; replace with a photo of the actual consultation sketchbook or a work-in-progress cake on the bench.
